Off-page SEO Checklist

Off-page SEO builds trust for your site from other places on the web. Links and mentions tell search engines your site is worth ranking.

ChecklistGood linksCommon mistakesFAQCase studiesTechnical SEO
⭐

Quality backlinks

One link from a relevant, trusted site beats many weak ones. Aim for sites your audience actually reads.

Quick check: check the referring domains in Semrush for relevance.
⚓

Anchor text

Mix branded, natural and keyword anchors. Too many exact keywords looks manipulated.

Quick check: no single anchor dominates the profile.
📣

Brand mentions

Get your name and site mentioned in articles, forums and communities. Mentions build trust, even without a link.

Quick check: search your brand name once a month.
👤

Profiles and listings

Link to your site from LinkedIn, GitHub and business listings. These are easy links that also help people find you.

Quick check: every profile uses the same name and URL.
✍️

Guest posts

Publish useful articles on relevant sites. Pick quality over volume and write something worth reading.

Quick check: each post fits the host site's audience.
📡

Link monitoring

Watch new and lost links and remove harmful ones. A regular check catches problems early.

Quick check: review backlinks every month.
🔎

Unlinked mentions

Sometimes sites mention your brand without linking to you. Find them with a brand search or Semrush, then politely ask for a link. It is one of the easiest links to win.

Quick check: search your brand name and check the first three pages.
🧹

Disavow with care

Google says most sites never need the disavow tool. Use it only for clear spam links you cannot remove, or after a manual action, never just because a link looks small.

Quick check: confirm a link is spam before you disavow it.

Good link or weak link?

Check these five things before you chase any link.

CheckGood linkWeak link
RelevanceSame topic or audienceUnrelated site
AuthorityTrusted site with real trafficNew site, no traffic, link farm
PlacementEditorial link inside the contentFooter, sidebar or sitewide
Anchor textNatural and variedExact keyword repeated
Link typeFollowed, or labelled honestly (nofollow, sponsored)Paid link with no sponsored label

Common mistakes

  • Buying cheap links in bulk.
  • Using the same anchor text everywhere.
  • Ignoring lost links.
  • Chasing quantity instead of relevance.
  • Not keeping up with the links your competitors earn.

Frequently asked questions

What is off-page SEO?

Off-page SEO is everything done outside your website to build trust, mainly backlinks, brand mentions and profiles.

Are nofollow links useless?

No. They still bring visitors and brand visibility, and Google treats nofollow as a hint, so they fit a natural link profile.

Should I use the disavow tool?

Rarely. Google says most sites do not need it. Use it only for clear spam links you cannot remove, especially after a manual action.

How many backlinks do I need?

There is no fixed number. Compare with the sites ranking above you and focus on relevance and quality.
